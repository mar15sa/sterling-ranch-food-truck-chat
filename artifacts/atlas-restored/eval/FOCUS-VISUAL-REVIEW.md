# Focus and separated layers review — September 21, 2026

Coordinator and final visual reviewer: GPT-6 Astra. Bounded independent screenshot review and test support: GPT-5.6 Terra. This is a separate 4190 local preview; 4189 and earlier comparisons remain unchanged. Owner approval and production launch remain pending.

## Requested behavior

Village buttons move the camera toward the extent of positioned destinations in that village; map place buttons move closer to that place. The surroundings soften through a feathered blur. Whole Ranch clears the camera treatment and restores the complete saved window. The focus region is illustration orientation, not a surveyed village boundary. Unlocated entries do not acquire coordinates.

Unfold now separates detailed destination models, CAB walking guides and future-project links into three independently animated planes above the existing terrain image. Each plane opens the actual corresponding content. Desktop planes remain separate; phone uses a vertically scrolling composition. This is an illustrated information stack, not physical building floors or a georeferenced exploded model.

Coming soon uses a dedicated future-only panel from both entry points. The current map, destination strip, current-place directory and current search results are excluded. All 11 groups and 16 future records remain. The source data and artwork are unchanged.

## Actual review

The unchanged detailed assets retain the architecture, landscaping and lighting of the pinned base. The full-area overview still shows the complete saved window and Prospect.

Terra independently reviewed the supplied desktop village/place focus, phone village focus, both Unfold states and phone future-only capture. It found no blocking overlap, clipping or current-content leak. It confirmed readable focused artwork, distinct actionable layers, and normal continuation below the phone viewport. This was a bounded screenshot review, not an independent browser certification.

During development, an Unfold heading overlapped the first plane; spacing was increased before the independent review. A rapid automated click while the camera was returning targeted a different control beneath the moving marker. Pointer activation on markers and the side card is now disabled during the camera transition, with normal activation restored on transition end/cancel. Subsequent place selection, source switch and reset were checked after the motion settled. Reduced-motion styles bypass the animations and transitions; an OS-level reduced-motion setting was not emulated.

The phone place-focus view also showed an unrelated landmark label clipped at the map edge. Place focus now displays only the selected marker, while village focus retains the relevant village markers. The final place-focus capture verifies the correction.
