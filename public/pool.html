Color key

What each CAB signal means

  • Open Green Light: open for homeowners and guests.
  • Temporarily closed Yellow Light: closed for weather or maintenance, with staff ready to reopen when possible.
  • Closed for the day Red Light: no access for homeowners or guests.
  • Event access only Purple Light: only registered event attendees can use the pool.
  • Open, but at capacity Blue Light: join the waitlist before entering.

Before you go

Closures

The pool may close for lightning, weather, maintenance, contamination, or capacity.
