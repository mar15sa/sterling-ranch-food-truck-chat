The affected family is all four scoped areas: payments/process/contact; property changes and governing amendments; utilities/equipment; facilities/events.

Before: relevant sources can be identified but their claims cannot answer, and the dashboard makes the inventory look like the active answer library. After: each remaining source has a documented content decision, useful current claims/actions are installed with exact version proof, obsolete/superseded/irrelevant claims are excluded, and the admin distinguishes inventory from active evidence.

## Review and authority

Three independent review partitions use category priority property changes, then utilities, then remaining operations; overlapping URLs have exactly one reviewer. The coordinator reconciles every original pending URL. Each reviewer reads current official content, records complete text and version, checks authority and replacement/currentness, and identifies supported claims and action proofs. A successful download alone does not establish currentness. Governing rules control binding requirements; official pages explain current process/operations; live systems supply time-sensitive availability/events; actions do not establish fees or permissions.
