This preview covers seven destinations around Sterling Center and The Overlook. It is not the full Sterling Ranch development or a property boundary map.

Shapes and location

Roads, building footprints, parks, pools and paths use the OpenStreetMap snapshot saved September 13, 2026. It is incomplete and has not been surveyed. Building heights, facade details, roofs and planting are illustrative.

Walking routes

The green map layer shows mapped paths and sidewalks. CAB’s route diagrams are shown separately on their official map, with its printed distances. They are not GPS navigation, verified accessible routes or live closure notices.

Places and what’s next

Visit details retain their official sources. New Overlook, Providence Park, trail, school and library notes were reviewed September 21, 2026. Existing directory records retain their earlier review dates. Hours and access can change; use the operator links before visiting.

Future sites have no guessed building footprints or entrance markers. Opening a roof reveals a directory, not a verified interior floor plan.
