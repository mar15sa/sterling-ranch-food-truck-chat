ABOUT THIS ATLAS

Real places. An illustrated world.

In the illustrated overview, the four landmark labels follow their painted models. Other markers provide approximate orientation. “Check map placement” switches all markers to the saved directory coordinates. The illustration uses the saved street layout as a guide; painted shapes, planting, buildings and finishes are approximate. “Check map placement” reveals the underlying source drawing.

This view includes the entire saved atlas area: Providence, Ascent, Prospect, Parkvale and the surrounding corridors. It is a map window, not a surveyed development boundary. All 25 current destinations and 101 saved listings are included; seven destinations without confirmed coordinates appear in the directory rather than receiving invented pins.

The opened models show a place’s businesses and amenities. The group positions do not describe rooms, entrances or suite locations. CAB’s walking guides remain separate source diagrams, not turn-by-turn navigation.
