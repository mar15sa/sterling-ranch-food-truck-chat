## Root causes and resident outcomes

Before this work, a completed URL inventory was mistaken for a completed answer library. New eligible sources also exposed shared selection defects: a broad directory could provide an unrelated contact; the word usage could make a plant calculation look like account-access help; retrieved neighbors could become citations/actions for an already-grounded rule answer; and process/report requests could be confused with live scheduling or binding rules.

The implementation validates each claim against its own exact quotation, converts metadata to the shared fact/action contracts, and keeps approval scope in data. Answer selection must preserve the resident's requested subject, process and authority. It cannot use a related word or a page's overall approval to supply an unsupported claim.

Final integration checks extended this boundary to shared task words: internet billing cannot borrow a water-billing contact or payment link, utility infrastructure fees cannot become clubhouse rental fees, and pool-party wording cannot borrow a Great Hall booking answer. Evaluation time is propagated consistently through fallback searches. The synthetic date used by the new review-family tests is scoped to that suite so it cannot expire unrelated fixtures when the complete test runner loads files together.

Held-out checks also require the requested entity to survive retrieval: one water system's findings cannot answer for another, an explicitly requested finding year cannot borrow another year, and compound requests must retain each requested system. Report publication dates remain separate from the period described by their findings. Caregiver, resident, nonresident, Great Hall and pavilion claims remain separate. Approved booking actions retain priority over general information links. Eligibility and quantity requests must be supported by the selected, rendered reviewed claim or remain explicitly incomplete. Full selected claims retain their conclusions and exceptions rather than being cut off at a character limit. Equipment-check instructions and plant-calculation methods remain distinct from live status, dates and binding requirements.

The complete regression gate also caught a compatibility gap in subject matching: older approved records sometimes label a claim only as phone, email, or weekday hours. Inferred topic labels must not become hard entity boundaries. The corrected boundary uses explicit reviewed subject labels for the already-approved report, facility and membership claims, while preserving legacy approvals. Fourteen existing claims received only narrower subject metadata; their exact quotations, source versions, actions and approval scopes did not change. Topic metadata helps retrieval but supplies no answer content. Genuinely partial operational answers have a distinct diagnostic mode as well as an incomplete status. A compound historical request with an unsupported system/year combination fails closed rather than treating another system's matching year as a complete answer; separate supported single-system questions remain answerable.

Canonical approved projections now require a valid future source expiry, including when a ledger copy exists. Newly reviewed PDFs opt into exact binary fingerprint checks: an image-only change fails closed even if extracted text is unchanged. Existing dynamic connector behavior is preserved. Changed or unavailable evidence does not inherit approval.

The broader answer audit also found an action-only support link ending recovery questions too early. Recovery and troubleshooting requests now continue to grounded guidance when the proposed shortcut has no reviewed explanation. A support destination remains a useful next step, not proof of a reset procedure, restored access or successful repair. This preserves the existing support evidence without approving new contact details or inventing device instructions.

## Owner view

The category cards distinguish evidence, links, live feeds and exclusions. Expanding a category shows all primary documents and pages; expanding a source shows its allowed details and limits. Expanded categories use the full available width. The question log's answer-accuracy section is collapsible.

Current evidence, completed inventory, content decisions and an external source-change queue are separate signals. An unavailable queue displays Unknown rather than zero. Candidate extraction conflicts are separated from conflicts involving approved evidence. In the reviewed bundle, the remaining 30 conflict groups concern unapproved extraction records, not new approved facts.

## Verification and release boundary

The review package, inventory reconciliation, exact PDF identity, stale canonical projections, typed contacts/methods, action proof and admin presentation have dedicated tests. Package-derived question-family tests exercise supported answers, current-safety exclusions, unrelated contacts, changed hashes and expiry. All hosted requests must include `isTest: true`; browser questions require `/community-assistant?test=1` and its visible Test mode banner.

The release bridge also rechecks approved versions due to expire within the next 30 minutes, so a source cannot age out halfway through the bounded quality run. Successful renewal uses the actual check time and requires the same live exact-version, action and document proof. The pre-existing baseline-identical outage-grace path is unchanged and reported separately as grace, not successful live verification; this candidate's recorded live recheck used no grace. Runtime refresh defaults and approval rules are unchanged. Unit tests, source checks and answer audits all use the same explicitly selected temporary revalidated snapshot. Loading that snapshot does not renew or approve anything, and a missing explicit snapshot fails rather than silently selecting old evidence.

The final controlling-source gate is tied to the completed approval decisions, not the earlier review backlog. The current rental and public pickleball projections can answer only within their matching approved versions and claims. The action-only internet-service page still cannot supply a phone or email; its explicit missing-contact boundary must not borrow the general directory. These expectation updates follow independent approval checks and preserve negative checks for unsupported claims and substituted sources.
