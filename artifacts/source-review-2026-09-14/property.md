The current signed 1979 policy provides fuller notice/hearing and cure-period requirements. Its fine amounts happen to agree; the process narrative must not be silently merged.

No new flag approval is proposed; the conflict must remain visible in the existing rule-answer path.

6. **Illustrated sheets:** the 2019 fence drawing has dimensions absent from extracted text. The storm-door sheet's shared fee note covers multiple projects and conflicts with exemptions elsewhere. These are visual/reference resources rather than independent complete rules or universal fees.
7. **Aggregate FAQs and redirects:** `/FAQ.aspx` and QID92 return category bundles containing obsolete CCMC quarterly billing, Smartstreet charges, 2020 account-launch and COVID-era text, and old retrofit credential requests.
