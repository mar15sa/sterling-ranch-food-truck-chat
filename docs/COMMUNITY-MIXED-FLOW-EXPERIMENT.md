### Evidence that changes the next action

1. **Understanding reliability precedes writer choice.** All four candidate yoga follow-up attempts failed the plan contract: the single need omitted its required request text. Both writers therefore received no answer-writing opportunity. The next controlled comparison should test the interpretation stage itself, including a model-only Sonnet replay, before selecting a cheap planner by assumption. Current-local yoga responses also withheld a date; neither path demonstrated success for this family.
2. **Source selection wastes cost and introduces distraction.** Food-menu packets included commercial-vehicle and food/drink rules, although those do not establish the named truck's menu. The full static catalog also adds unrelated topics. Correct-role retrieval needs a controlled comparison; a more expensive writer does not justify irrelevant evidence. The real food listing was available, but verified menu details were absent.
3. **The stronger writer helped some distinctions.** In the recorded lighting follow-up, Opus began with the distinction between permanent installation and seasonal operating settings; both Sonnet trials began with a contradictory “No” before discussing operation, and one later acknowledged the fixtures could remain. Opus shed drafts preserved the height exception and explicitly acknowledged that the specific form was not identified. These are developer observations on known cases, not blind human scores or proof of every claim.
4. **A verified status still does not mean a useful answer.** The current control's recycling/storage response was marked verified and complete but omitted the next pickup date and included unrelated Ascent landscaping prose. Its pool/status-hours replies withheld the answer, while the candidates used current status and approved seasonal hours. Such cases must count against usefulness instead of being rewarded for formatting or source presence.
5. **Speed misses the target.** Ten of twelve trials in each candidate arm exceeded ten seconds, even without inline grading. Both unselected candidates need less unnecessary work and more reliable planning. Added serial checking would require measured benefit and a new full cost/latency total.

The developer audit is not exhaustive: final usefulness/excellence and all material claim checks remain unestablished. Live feeds were queried per trial and could vary; retained packets and timestamps must be checked before assigning causal model gains. Original request bodies and normalized responses were saved. Rejected interpretation records retain normalized needs/validation issues but discard the raw live-binding object during resolution; this capture cannot prove the complete original provider tool object. Future interpretation diagnostics should retain it explicitly.
